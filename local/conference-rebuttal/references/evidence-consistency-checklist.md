# Rebuttal evidence checklist

Before finalizing, check each substantive response:

- The paper section/table/figure or supplied receipt actually supports the
  stated claim.
- Every number, metric direction, dataset split, model, prompt, and setting
  matches the submitted paper or is clearly labelled as additional evidence.
- An additional experiment states its setting, control, metric, and result; a
  planned experiment is not written as completed.
- Limitations and uncertainty are bounded rather than turned into a broader
  concession than the reviewer made.
- Every reviewer concern is covered, or an explicit reason for deferral is
  recorded.
- Citations are real, relevant, allowed by the venue, and not used to replace
  the direct answer.
- Identity and links follow the current stage's rules; private paths,
  credentials, and confidential material are excluded from the response.
- The final text fits the venue's word/format limit and contains no unsupported
  promise about code, data, or future results.
