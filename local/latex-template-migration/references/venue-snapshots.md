# Official venue snapshots (starting points)

These are dated examples, not universal rules. Re-open the linked official
pages for the target track and record the access date in the project memo before
using any value. Rules can change after this snapshot.

Snapshot checked: 2026-09-03 (Asia/Hong_Kong).

## ICML 2026

Sources: [Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions),
[Call for Papers](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers), and the
[official style/example files](https://media.icml.cc/Conferences/ICML2026/Styles/example_paper.pdf).

- Submission uses LaTeX and a single PDF; the main body is at most 8 pages,
  with references, impact statement, and appendices after it. The submission
  PDF limit is 50 MB; the camera-ready limit is 20 MB.
- The process is double-blind. The submitted paper and supplementary material
  must be anonymized; prior work should be cited in third person, without
  acknowledgements, grant numbers, or non-anonymous repository links.
- Supplementary manuscripts and anonymized code/data are allowed, but material
  critical to evaluation belongs in the main paper. There is no separate
  supplementary deadline.
- ICML main-track papers require a broader-impact statement and may impose
  reciprocal-reviewer obligations. The position-paper track has explicit
  exceptions; check the [position-paper call](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPositionPapers)
  and current submission form rather than copying these policies into a generic
  checklist.

## ICLR 2027

Sources: [Call for Papers](https://www.iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers),
[Author Guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines), and
the [official style files](https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip).

- The submission is double-blind and uses OpenReview. The 2027 author
  guidelines state a main-text limit of 9 pages at submission, unlimited
  references, and optional appendices after the bibliography; reviewers need
  not read appendices or supplementary material.
- Supplementary text should be clearly marked after the references; code and
  other supplementary files are optional and should be treated as review
  evidence, not a replacement for essential main-text evidence.
- An AI-use statement is required and does not count toward the page limit;
  an ethics statement is recommended. Author additions stop at the abstract
  deadline, and title changes are restricted after the paper deadline.
- Current quota, co-authorship, reciprocal-reviewing, and AI-policy details
  are part of the live author guidelines; record them as venue-specific fields
  rather than assuming they apply to another conference.

## Use in a project memo

For either venue, copy only confirmed values into the checklist. Keep separate
rows for main-text pages, references, appendices, supplementary files, identity
leaks, metadata/source ZIP, required statements, deadlines, and submission-form
obligations. Unknown or conflicting rules remain Open Questions until resolved
from an official source or confirmed by the user.
